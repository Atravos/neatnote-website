From the developer

Built by one person.
On purpose.

NeatNote started at my desk at work. I used Notepad++ as a scratchpad — pasting snippets, jotting down tasks — and it slowly turned into dozens of untitled tabs I couldn't find anything in. Notion and Evernote were far too heavy for that. So I built what I actually wanted: something as quick as a text file, with a sidebar of folders for the things worth keeping.

There's no company behind NeatNote. No investors, no growth team, no roadmap meeting deciding how to squeeze you into a subscription. It's one developer, a public GitHub repo, and people who think good software is worth ten bucks. Every purchase directly funds the next update.
