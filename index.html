Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want to write — not configure, learn, or babysit their notes app.

Clean, rich formatting

Headings, bold and italic, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, code blocks and links — from a simple toolbar or the shortcuts you already know.

Folders, your way

Nest folders as deep as you like. Drag to reorder or move, right-click to create, rename or reveal. Your layout is remembered between sessions.

Find anything

Search looks inside every note, not just titles, and shows the line that matched. Ctrl/⌘ + P and start typing.

Saves as you type

Changes are saved automatically the moment you pause. Close the window mid-sentence and it's still there. Deleted notes go to your system trash, so they can be restored.

Private by design

No cloud, no account, no telemetry. NeatNote never connects to the internet unless you allow it — even spell check works offline. Your notes are ordinary files you can open, copy and back up.

Never locked in

Export any note to Markdown in one click and take it anywhere. And because NeatNote is open source, your app can't disappear on you either.
