Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

The short version

The NeatNote app collects nothing. Your notes stay on your device. The only personal data we ever receive is your email address when you buy the app — and we only use it to send you your download links, let you know about NeatNote updates, and respond if you contact us.

The NeatNote app

NeatNote is a local-first application. Everything you write is stored on your own device, in files you control.

  • No accounts. The app never asks you to sign up or log in.
  • No cloud storage. Your notes are never uploaded to our servers — we don't have servers that store notes.
  • No telemetry or analytics. The app does not track how you use it, and it does not send usage data, crash reports, or any other information back to us.
  • No connections unless you allow them. The first time it opens, the app asks whether it may check for updates. Until you say yes, it doesn't connect to the internet at all. If you allow it, each time it opens it asks GitHub (where NeatNote is published) whether a newer version exists. That request asks for public release information only and contains nothing about you or your notes — though, as with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Updates, or press Check for updates yourself instead. If you choose to install an update on Windows, the installer is downloaded from GitHub too. The app makes no other connections. Spell check uses dictionaries that ship with the app (or with your operating system on macOS), so nothing is downloaded. Links in your notes open in your own web browser, only when you choose to open them.

NeatNote is open source, so you don't have to take our word for it — you can verify these claims by reading the code on GitHub.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. When you make a purchase, Stripe shares your email address and order details with us so we can deliver your download links and honor refunds. Stripe's handling of your payment information is governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

This website

This website is a static site. We do not run advertising trackers or marketing cookies. We use GoatCounter to count visits. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites, and doesn't collect personal information. It only records things like which page was viewed, the site you came from, your browser and screen size, and your country. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers receive your IP address when the page loads, as with any externally hosted resource.

Email

If you email us at theneatnoteapp@gmail.com, we use your email address only to respond to you. We don't add you to mailing lists or share your address with anyone.

Data requests

Because the app stores nothing with us, the only data we may hold about you is a purchase record (email address and order details). If you'd like it deleted or want a copy of it, email us and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Since the app doesn't collect data, we don't expect it to change much.
